About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology and Maritime Port (AASTMT), with a passion for cybersecurity that was sparked by playing Watch Dogs.

Currently ranked in the top ~850 on Hack The Box with Hacker status, I spend my time solving CTF challenges, writing detailed walkthroughs, and preparing for the OSCP certification following TJNull's methodology.

My expertise spans privilege escalation, SQL injection, Linux enumeration, networking protocols, and offensive security techniques. I believe in learning through hands-on practice and sharing knowledge with the community.
